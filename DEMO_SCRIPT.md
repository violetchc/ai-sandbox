# Five-minute OCS walkthrough

Use mock mode. All data is local and fictional; no API key is needed.

**0:00–0:35 — Start with a task.** Open Explore. Notice career filters and curated capabilities rather than a news feed. Choose Internal PRD Maker and load the demo idea. Explain that students bring a real task while avoiding private records.

**0:35–1:20 — Capture and draft.** Click Structure my idea. Show the completeness meter and the missing baseline. In Metrics, write `Baseline: 20 minutes; target: 14 minutes per weekly update; measure over three pilot weeks.` Click Generate PRD. Expand Requirements to show IDs, priorities, rationale and acceptance criteria. Edit Overview, move focus outside the field, and compare versions.

**1:20–2:05 — Check policies.** Open Check the policies and run the check. Show the exact draft excerpt and illustrative clause. Apply one suggested fix. Explain that recording a control does not prove compliance; a person must review it. Do not check human sign-off just to improve the score.

**2:05–2:55 — Practice pushback.** Open Pressure-test the plan. Select a few departments and run review and debate. Increase hostility or enable devil's advocate, then rerun to compare severity. Answer an objection: `The engineering lead will verify the acceptance criteria with a test before the pilot.` Show the structure score, debate and heatmap. Export the risk register if useful.

**2:55–3:40 — Check the resources.** Open Make a realistic timeline. Confirm editable estimates and hours, then Generate resource plan. Remove one engineer to see infeasibility, restore the plan, and reduce P2 scope or move the deadline. Explain that the dates come from deterministic capacity calculations, not language-model arithmetic.

**3:40–4:20 — Share honestly.** Open Prepare to share. Show the checklist and blockers. Items remain open until there is evidence and a human owner. Export the PRD, timeline or checklist; Print / Save as PDF gives a reviewable text record.

**4:20–5:00 — Turn practice into proof.** Expand the learning panel's manual-vs-AI comparison. Enter actual times and quality ratings from a practice attempt; save to Proof Log. Download starter SKILL.md. Open My portfolio and export the interview story. It distinguishes delegated work, personal judgment, prompting decisions and measured results without claiming actual departmental approval.
